# ClaritasZ Reports — Independent Source Scan Rule

Status: binding editorial ground  
Applies to: all Dutch, European, international, weekly and monthly ClaritasZ report editions

## Rule

Every edition begins with a fully independent source scan within its own geographic scope and reporting period.

A previous edition must never be used as a source list, shortlist, search direction or search seed for another edition. The publication date of the preceding edition in the same series may be used only to establish the reporting window.

The editor first forms a provisional candidate set independently from current, authoritative primary sources. Previous editions are consulted only after that candidate set exists, and solely to:

1. identify repetition;
2. establish whether status, normative effect, risk or decision impact demonstrably changed;
3. explain any independently selected overlap.

Overlap is permitted only when a development independently crosses the materiality threshold in each applicable scan. Its geographic meaning and distinct decision impact must then be explicit.

Difference is not forced. Independence of observation is required.

## Scope by edition

### Dutch weekly edition

Perform a separate Dutch scan. Select developments with material impact in the Netherlands. European or international developments qualify only when their concrete Dutch effect can be established.

### European weekly edition

Perform a separate European scan. Do not translate, broaden or reuse the Dutch selection. National developments qualify only when they have material European meaning.

### International edition

Perform a separate worldwide scan across multiple regions. Do not treat the European selection as its starting point and then add non European subjects. European developments qualify only when their global materiality is independently established.

### Monthly review

Reassess the complete month independently. Do not assemble the monthly selection by combining or ranking weekly selections. Weekly editions may be consulted only after the provisional monthly candidate set has been formed.

## Shared factual ground within an edition

The six architecture perspectives within one edition use the same confirmed factual ground. Selection is shared only within that edition. Interpretation, tension, sector impact and decision relevance remain specific to each architecture responsibility.

## Insufficient evidence

A thin first search is not a reason to borrow subjects from another edition. Broaden the scan within the edition's own scope. If the materiality threshold still cannot be met, publish fewer developments and state the evidence gap.

## Editorial record

Working notes should preserve:

1. geographic scope and reporting window;
2. source domains and categories scanned;
3. provisional candidate set;
4. materiality grounds for selection and rejection;
5. comparison with the previous edition performed after provisional selection;
6. explanation of any overlap with another edition.

This record supports traceability. It does not predetermine the outcome of the scan.
